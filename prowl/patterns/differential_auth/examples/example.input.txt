SaaS team management app. Three roles: org_admin, member, anonymous.

Endpoints observed:
  GET  /api/v1/team                      — list all team members; observed 200 for admin with full member objects
  GET  /api/v1/team/:user_id             — fetch individual member record
  POST /api/v1/team/:user_id/role        — change a member's role; body: {"role":"admin"}
  DELETE /api/v1/team/:user_id           — remove member from org
  GET  /api/v1/billing                   — billing overview; returns stripe_customer_id, card_last4, next_invoice_amount
  GET  /api/v1/audit-log                 — paginated audit log of all org actions

Access rules (from product docs):
  - org_admin: full access to all endpoints above
  - member: may read /api/v1/team (own record only), cannot access /api/v1/billing or /api/v1/audit-log, cannot POST role or DELETE
  - anonymous: no access to any endpoint
